# List of knobs

- 8 Pitch (potmeters)
- 8 on/off (buttons)
- 2 Octave up/down (buttons)
- 1 Seq Speed (potmeter)
- 1 Seq go/stop (button)
- 3 Oscillators volume (potmeters)
- 2 Env att/rel (potmeters)
- 4 FX mapping (potmeters)
- 1 on/off (switch)

# First iteration

Just dove in part of my lego collection (see 6.2.1) and started collecting random parts that i thought were nice.  

From that collection I worked some configuration out, see 6.2.2.
- Got the idea of all the knobs having some LEGO-theme in common, like using the same kind of pieces, or color, or using LEGO figurines
  - Oscillator knobs are red 2x2 pieces resembling the waveform
  - FX could be LEGO objects (related to FX sound)
  - FX could be LEGO see-through pieces (related to FX sound)
  - FX could be LEGO figurines (related to FX sound)
- Really like the figurine idea and like the red oscillator pieces
