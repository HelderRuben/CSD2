## Rotary encoders
*These need rotary encoder:*   
- 8 Pitch (potmeters)  

Doing some research, i found cheap ones with 20 steps which would mean an octave + fifth in pitch range

I had 13-step in my mind (octave) and I guess i could make the choice of not having the encoder feel, but programming the non-encoder potmeter to round to an increment for pitch, but i don't really like that bc it doesn't have the feel.  

Nevermind, just found out that you can just keep on turning the encoder, so 20-step is fine and i'll program it to be 13-step.  

Also worth mentioning, the rotary encoder has a knob built in! I can easily use that for turning on/the steps in the Seq
**So i'm doing the 20-step rotary encoders.**
