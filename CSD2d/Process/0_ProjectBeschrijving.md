**Short answer**  
I ambolutely love LEGO.  
**Long answer**  
When I was making some wiz-of-oz prototypes, I build one out of LEGO because it is easy to build and take apart, and I also enjoy building with it. It felt a good building material. ## Why is there no Text on the Sequencer?
**No Labels**  
There are no labels on the sequencer buttons saying "this is what this button does". I wanted to add some creative spark in this.  
**Why?**  
When working with audio plug-ins, I always find interfaces without text so creative. I enjoy working with it because it trains my brain to think in concepts instead of "tweak technical things to the max". When the knob "does a sound characteristic", it works better for me than when it "tweaks this literal technical thing".  
**How is there no Text then?**  
The visual representation of the functions of the knobs are realised by giving a conceptual visual image of what characteristic the knob adds to the sound.  
**How did I realise this?**  
This went really well with the vision to create the end result (shell) out of LEGO. For there are no words in LEGO, but still you can interpret pieces in a creative way.  
**Important About This:**  
This project, I am experimenting with this. I did not do a complete research for how this works and how to interpret different visual images of what makes the sound. It is something I am very interested in, but at least for right now it is not the Main Goal.
