# This Block Takeaways
*This chapter will keep being updated throughout the project. This immediately fulfils takeaway 11 from last block.*

## 1: Quality instead of Quantity (documenting things)
Don't write so much without saying anything, still learning that. Especially with documenting ideas (see step 2, this was way too much and not necessary).  

## 2: Doing instead of thinking (making and little tests)
In lesson 1, I got this feedback and felt it important to write down:  
Don't spend so much time working out ideas without a tangible test or a Wiz-of-Oz. First think about it conceptually, quickly braindump the idea and then do a short small test to 'feel' the idea.  

## 3: Involve others (ideas and interaction)
To continue on takeaway #2, test these little test on others also! I had joy in testing my small wiz-of-oz on others, to see what they would do.  

## 4: More Prototypes
To come to an end product I needed more prototypes than I anticipated (and still do). I am now very conscious of the fact that every step is something that needs refining, and the ideas inside my head almost never work instantly.  

## 5: Soldering is fun!
As I expected, I want to do even more with hardware now.
