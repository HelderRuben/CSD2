# Diverging more: Why?

After the first lesson, I came to the realisation (due to feedback) that I was very much working out ideas to the details, but it was too soon for that in the process. Even the little cards I made weren't "one-minute-ideas", but i thought about them. I need to let go of "oh is this even possible with the hardware?" and just let creativity bring me somewhere. Also, nice feedback was that i need to think about the idea physically and test it or Oz it. Because if i don't do that and i work out an idea to the details, it might not even have a goal, let alone be appealing to work with. I learned that when it comes to ideas, i don't have to take it slow or work them out, just go quick.

So:  
- Diverge more: more little short ideas, combining them, taking steps in the creative-idea-space
- Imagining and/or Ozzing the ideas to pick out which ones are actually nice

ThinkPak:  
- Read Eliminate cards:
  - I can minimalize my ideas to make interaction simple
  - I can minimalize my ideas to get to the core concept of the interaction(the goal)
- Read Combine cards:
  - I can expand my ideas by combining characteristics of different ideas
- Read Reverse cards:
  - I can improve my creativity by thinking about something unexpected
  - I can improve my ideas by thinking about what the opposite of the idea would be
- Read Put to Other Uses card:
  - I can expand on the idea by exploring different uses
  - I can expand on the idea by exploring possible spinoffs
- Read Adapt cards:
  - I can gather new insights by listing connections between my idea and other random objects or ideas
- Read Modify cards:
  - I can expand my perspective on the idea by listing (small or big) things i can modify
    - This is something i'd like to work out at in the concrete design-phase
- Read Substitute cards:
  - I can expand my perspective on the idea by experimenting with substituting different parts or approaches
- Read Magnify cards:
  - I can expand my idea by imagining the extreme of certain aspects
- Read Rearrange cards:
  - I can work out my idea on a physical level by thinking about the order in which components and functions are placed  
- Read Evaluate cards:
  - Don't evaluate ideas during the brainstorming, it kills creativity
  - Be a critic about your own ideas in evaluating them and improve them
