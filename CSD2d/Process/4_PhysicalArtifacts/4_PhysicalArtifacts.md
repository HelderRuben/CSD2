# Testing with Physical Artifacts

I diverged more in the ideation phase by brain dumping quick ideas on paper, but I also want something tangible to test and to feel the idea itself. I did some tests with random things in my room, and also with lego. In this MD i want to focus on the conclusions i drew from these tests.

## Takeaways from each Physical Artifact

- 4.1: Nice to have control over knobs with specific effects, feels nice
- 4.2: Seeing these effects as different parts is not attractive to me
- 4.3.1 en 4.3.2: It was fun giving these blocks a try but it felt too complex to even be an option. I had the thougth "this is unnessecarily complicated"  
- 4.4: Only having 2 knobs as options feels pretty comfortable actually
- 4.5.1: Synth is nice! I like having control of pitch in a scale. buttons are nice for synth
- 4.5.2: Synth expansion pack. I like having the control
- 4.6: pressure plates ideas was nice but actually i do not like playing with it at all... crossing that off the list
- 4.7: I really like the idea of the 8-step sequencer! I love playing with it with the sounds in my head, and having control over the pitch, octave, and envelope

## General Takeaways that impact my design-proces

1. **LEGO**: Building with lego was so much fun that i want to incorporate lego into the design of the synth. This is were I got the idea of a 'themed' synth, with the theme of lego and the knobs somehow doing this related to lego and the pieces that they represent
2. **Knobs with not-directly-musical terms**: 4.4 made me realise that one knob can do a lot (also in combination with the previous block). I think it is very fun to have knobs with titles that have no concrete musical context like "threshold" or "reverb", but more conceptual like "lightning" or "atmosphere". For instance, I love Lofi Oddity (FX plugin) and it has a knob with an unreadable name, which does some pretty cool stuff.
This reminded me of an assignment i did last year (making a vital preset with 2 macro-knobs), and i can use that one for wizard-of-ozzing this!

## Ozzing

I made a LEGO-build with the macro knobs of the Vital preset i made last year
**4.8**: Was very fun trying this out on people! I tested it at home on 5 people, short list of what happened:  
I just invited them, said they were a test person, put the build in front of them, pressed play and watched what they did.  
- Some tried pressing the 'buttons' on the side of the build
- Some lifted the build
- Some tried telling me to stop the sound, thinking the test was only listening and watching
- Most of them (eventually or instantly) got the idea of the pieces being the knobs, and played with them for a while before telling me they were satisfied  
- All of them first listened for an amount of time before doing something
